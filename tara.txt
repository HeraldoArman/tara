# TARA — Project Context & Product Brief

## 1. Project Overview

**Product Name:** TARA  
**Positioning:** AI-Powered Operational Intelligence & Workforce Assistant  
**Tagline:** Intelligence in Every Operation.  
**Context:** Sea × OpenAI Regional Codex Hackathon Indonesia 2026.

TARA is an AI-powered platform that transforms static Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) into intelligent, executable workflows.

It connects two sides of business operations:

1. **Management Layer (Head):** Enables company heads to upload and manage SOPs, understand AI opportunities within their operational processes, and monitor whether employees are following established procedures.
2. **Execution Layer (Employee):** Provides frontline workers with a real-time AI assistant that guides them through operational tasks, observes their actions, verifies critical checkpoints, and helps them correct mistakes.

The central idea is to close the gap between what an SOP says and what actually happens during operational execution.

TARA is not simply an SOP summarizer, checklist application, generic chatbot, or object detection system. Its differentiation lies in connecting SOP understanding, AI opportunity mapping, real-time visual assistance, deterministic verification, and operational monitoring.

---

## 2. The Problem

Companies often rely on static SOP documents, PDFs, manuals, and checklists to standardize operational processes.

However:
- Employees must interpret lengthy documents themselves.
- Critical steps and dependencies can be overlooked.
- Procedures may be executed incorrectly or out of sequence.
- Operational deviations may go unnoticed until after execution.
- Supervisors lack real-time visibility into SOP adherence.
- Operational evidence and corrective actions are often inconsistently recorded.
- Organizations may not understand which parts of their existing workflows could benefit from AI.

TARA addresses these issues by converting documented procedures into executable workflows and supporting employees during actual execution, while giving management visibility into operational performance.

## 3. Core Product Capabilities

### A. SOP Intelligence

Company heads can upload existing SOP documents, such as PDF or DOCX files.

TARA analyzes the documents and extracts:
- Workflow steps and task descriptions.
- Step dependencies and prerequisites.
- Expected outcomes.
- Critical checkpoints.
- Risks and safety requirements.
- Verification criteria.
- Exception-handling procedures.

The result is a structured workflow that can be reviewed and used for operational execution.

The uploaded SOP remains the source of truth. TARA should not invent requirements or verification criteria that are not grounded in the document.

### B. AI Opportunity Mapping

After understanding the SOP, TARA identifies opportunities to improve operational workflows through AI.

Each task can be categorized into:
1. Human-performed.
2. AI-assisted.
3. AI-verified.
4. Automated.
5. Human approval required.

TARA explains why a task could benefit from AI, which AI capability may be useful (e.g., computer vision, OCR, barcode recognition, LLM), and where human oversight is still necessary.

This feature helps companies understand how AI can improve their existing operations rather than simply adding AI for the sake of it.

### C. Real-Time AI Workforce Assistant

Employees use TARA while performing actual operational tasks.

The assistant:
- Displays the current SOP step.
- Provides contextual instructions.
- Observes the working environment through a webcam or camera.
- Detects relevant visual evidence using computer vision.
- Checks whether required conditions have been satisfied.
- Alerts employees when something is missing, incorrect, or out of sequence.
- Guides them through corrective actions.
- Re-verifies the task after correction.

The experience should be proactive and context-aware rather than a conventional chatbot conversation.

The assistant should focus on the current operational context and relevant instruction, not a traditional chat history.

### D. Operational Monitoring & Compliance

Company heads can monitor operational execution at a high level.

They should be able to understand:
- How many employees are executing assigned SOPs.
- Which workflows have been completed.
- Whether critical checkpoints have been verified.
- Which procedures have deviations.
- Which alerts require attention.
- Historical execution records and audit trails.
- Overall SOP adherence across operational activities.

The goal is to give management visibility into whether procedures are actually being followed, not merely whether employees have opened or started an SOP.

Compliance metrics must be grounded in actual verification records. Missing visual evidence should not automatically be classified as a violation.

## 4. Main User Roles

TARA has two primary user roles.

### Head / Company Management

Responsible for establishing and monitoring operational standards.

Main capabilities:
- Upload company SOPs.
- Review and manage extracted workflows.
- View AI Opportunity Mapping.
- Make workflows available to employees.
- Monitor workforce execution.
- Review compliance, deviations, and alerts.
- Access operational reports and audit records.

### Employee / Frontline Worker

Responsible for executing operational tasks.

Main capabilities:
- Access assigned SOPs and workflows.
- Start a task with TARA.
- Receive real-time contextual instructions.
- Use camera-assisted task verification.
- Respond to alerts and correct deviations.
- Complete tasks and access execution history.

Both roles use the same underlying SOP and operational data. Their access permissions and primary experiences differ.

## 5. Main User Flow

### A. Public Entry

1. User visits the TARA landing page.
2. Landing page explains the product and its value proposition.
3. User clicks the primary CTA, such as "Start TARA".
4. User is redirected to the login or sign-up page.
5. After authentication, TARA identifies the user's role.

### B. First-Time Access — Head

For a head accessing TARA for the first time:

1. Login / Sign Up.
2. Role identification as Head.
3. Initial SOP setup.
4. Upload an existing company SOP (PDF/DOCX).
5. TARA analyzes the document and generates a structured workflow.
6. TARA identifies critical checkpoints, risks, prerequisites, and verification criteria.
7. TARA generates an AI Opportunity Map.
8. Head reviews and confirms the generated workflow.
9. The workflow becomes available for operational execution.
10. Head enters the Admin Dashboard.

The initial setup only occurs when the company or head has not yet configured an SOP. Returning users should go directly to their dashboard.

### C. Returning Access — Head

1. Login.
2. Redirect to the Admin Dashboard.
3. View operational overview.
4. Monitor employee execution and SOP adherence.
5. Review deviations, alerts, and historical activity.
6. Upload new SOPs or update existing ones when needed.

### D. Employee Access

1. Login.
2. Redirect to Employee Home / My Tasks.
3. View available or assigned workflows.
4. Select an operational task.
5. Start TARA's Real-Time Assistant.
6. Execute the SOP with live assistance.
7. Receive verification results and corrective instructions.
8. Complete the workflow.
9. Execution records are saved and made available for management monitoring.

## 6. Real-Time Assistant — Operational State Concept

The real-time assistant has four main visual and operational states:

1. **Sleep:** TARA is idle or waiting for a task to begin.
2. **Active:** TARA is observing the environment and verifying the current SOP step.
3. **Warning:** TARA detects a mismatch, missing requirement, deviation, or condition that needs attention.
4. **Success:** A checkpoint has been successfully verified.

These states represent the current operational condition and should be reflected in the assistant's behavior.

A successful checkpoint should generally transition back to Active when the workflow continues. A fully completed workflow should have its own completion state.

### Two Assistant Modes

**Demo Mode**
- Designed for presentations and hackathon demonstrations.
- Camera feed is the primary focus.
- Contextual assistant instructions are displayed alongside the video.
- Makes it easy for observers to understand the relationship between visual observations and AI responses.

**Product Mode**
- Designed for actual employee use.
- Instructions and contextual guidance are the primary focus.
- Camera feed is secondary.
- Helps workers concentrate on the task and the next required action.

Both modes use the same underlying execution engine and verification logic.

## 7. Technical Architecture & AI Principles

TARA combines several technologies with clearly separated responsibilities.

### SOP Processing
- PDF/DOCX extraction.
- LLM-based semantic understanding.
- Structured workflow generation.
- JSON representation of tasks, dependencies, risks, and criteria.

### AI Opportunity Mapping
- LLM-based task analysis.
- Recommendation of appropriate AI capabilities.
- Identification of tasks requiring human oversight.

### Computer Vision
- Webcam-based visual observation.
- Object detection and classification.
- OCR and barcode recognition.
- Visual state detection.
- Temporal tracking where necessary.

### Deterministic SOP Execution Engine

This is a critical part of TARA.

The LLM must not independently decide whether a safety-critical procedure is compliant.

A deterministic state machine or rule engine evaluates:
- Prerequisites.
- Workflow order.
- Verification criteria.
- Evidence sufficiency.
- Exceptions.
- Conditions for advancing or blocking a step.

Possible states include:
- `pending`
- `in_progress`
- `verified`
- `not_evidenced`
- `contradicted`
- `needs_verification`
- `blocked`
- `requires_approval`

The system must distinguish between missing evidence and evidence that contradicts a requirement. If visual evidence is insufficient, TARA should request human verification instead of making unsupported claims.

### LLM Guidance

The LLM translates system states and SOP requirements into natural-language instructions.

It guides employees but does not own deterministic compliance decisions.

### Audit Trail

Record:
- Task initiation.
- Step transitions.
- Verification results.
- Alerts.
- Corrective actions.
- Approval events.
- Task completion.

Prefer storing relevant evidence snapshots and event records instead of continuously storing raw video by default.

## 8. Hackathon MVP Scope

The goal is to build one complete, reliable end-to-end operational scenario.

Essential capabilities:
1. Head login and initial SOP upload.
2. SOP extraction and workflow generation.
3. AI Opportunity Mapping.
4. Head dashboard with basic operational monitoring.
5. Employee login and workflow selection.
6. Real-time assistant with webcam access.
7. Detection of two or three relevant visual conditions.
8. Deterministic verification and workflow state transitions.
9. Contextual warnings and corrective instructions.
10. Completion summary and audit record.
11. Management visibility into employee execution results.

Prioritize reliability and a compelling demonstration over feature quantity.

Avoid unnecessary complexity such as:
- Complex enterprise RBAC.
- Multiple organizational hierarchies.
- Advanced analytics.
- Agent marketplaces.
- Fully autonomous agent execution.
- Multiple partially implemented operational scenarios.

## 9. Product Differentiation

TARA connects five capabilities:

1. **Understand:** Convert static SOP documents into structured operational workflows.
2. **Optimize:** Identify opportunities for AI assistance and automation.
3. **Observe:** Monitor relevant physical-world evidence during execution.
4. **Verify:** Evaluate task completion against deterministic SOP requirements.
5. **Monitor:** Give management visibility into execution quality, deviations, and compliance.

The central product thesis:

**TARA closes the gap between what an SOP says and what actually happens on the operational floor.**

It connects management's operational standards with employees' real-world execution.
